## What is the Personalised Aftercare Schedule Generator?

The Personalised Aftercare Schedule Generator is a free web tool that creates a phase-appropriate aftercare plan based on three inputs: piercing type, days since piercing, and current condition. The tool uses a clinical algorithm that maps each piercing type to its expected healing timeline across three phases: acute/inflammatory, proliferative/rebuilding, and remodelling/maturation.

When a user generates a schedule, the tool calculates which healing phase the piercing is currently in, displays a progress percentage toward full healing, and outputs a tailored routine with specific cleaning instructions, avoidance guidelines, and jewellery recommendations. The tool also provides condition-specific advice for bumps, irritation, or unusual discharge, following Association of Professional Piercers (APP) aftercare guidelines.

The generator covers 11 piercing categories including earlobe, ear cartilage, daith/rook/snug/tragus, nostril, septum, labret/lip/monroe, tongue, navel, surface/dermal, nipple, and genital piercings. Each type has unique healing day parameters: acute phase end, proliferative phase end, and total expected healing days, ranging from 120 days for tongue piercings to 365 days for cartilage, daith, navel, surface, nipple, and genital piercings.

## How to Use the Personalised Aftercare Schedule Generator

### Step 1: Select Your Piercing Type
Open the dropdown menu labelled "Piercing type" and choose the option that matches your piercing. The tool offers 11 categories:
- Earlobe
- Ear cartilage (helix/flat/conch)
- Daith/rook/snug/tragus
- Nostril
- Septum
- Labret/lip/monroe
- Tongue
- Navel/belly button
- Surface/dermal/microdermal
- Nipple
- Genital (general)

### Step 2: Enter Days Since Piercing
In the "Days since piercing" field, type the number of days since you received the piercing. The tool accepts values from 0 to 730 days. If you are unsure of the exact number, count from the piercing date to today.

### Step 3: Describe Current Condition
Select the option that best describes your piercing's current state:
- Normal: no concerns
- Small bump or raised tissue at site
- Redness, tenderness, or minor irritation
- Unusual discharge (not normal lymph fluid)

### Step 4: Generate Your Schedule
Click the "Generate My Aftercare Schedule" button. The tool will calculate your healing phase and display a complete schedule including:
- Phase badge and name
- Healing progress percentage bar
- Phase-specific aftercare routine steps
- What to avoid at this stage
- Jewellery recommendations

Scroll down to view the full results. The tool will automatically scroll to the results section after generation.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How does the tool determine which healing phase I am in?
The tool uses a predefined healing timeline for each piercing type. Each type has three values: acute phase end (days), proliferative phase end (days), and total healing days. The tool compares your entered days since piercing against these thresholds to determine your current phase.

### 2. What piercing types does the generator support?
The tool supports 11 categories: earlobe, ear cartilage, daith/rook/snug/tragus, nostril, septum, labret/lip/monroe, tongue, navel, surface/dermal, nipple, and genital piercings. Each category has its own healing timeline.

### 3. Can I use this tool if my piercing is over 2 years old?
Yes. The tool accepts values up to 730 days. If you enter a value beyond the total healing days for your piercing type, the tool will display the "Fully healed/Maintenance" phase with appropriate care instructions.

### 4. What should I do if the tool detects a condition like a bump or irritation?
The tool displays a condition-specific alert box with detailed advice. For bumps, it recommends increasing saline rinses and warm compresses. For irritation, it suggests reverting to basic saline-only care. For unusual discharge, it advises stopping all products except sterile saline and seeking medical assessment.

### 5. Does the tool replace professional medical advice?
No. The tool follows APP aftercare guidelines but includes a disclaimer stating that signs of infection (spreading redness, fever, pus, severe swelling) require prompt medical attention. The tool advises against relying on it for infection symptoms.

### 6. Why does the tool recommend against rotating jewellery?
Rotating jewellery disrupts fistula formation and creates microtrauma. The tool explicitly lists this as something to avoid in Phase 1 and Phase 2, as the healing channel is still forming and unstable.

### 7. What is BioFlex and why does the tool recommend it?
BioFlex is a biocompatible polymer jewellery material. The tool recommends it for movement-stressed placements like navel, tongue, genital, and surface piercings because its flexibility reduces chronic trauma during healing.

### 8. How accurate is the healing progress percentage?
The progress percentage is calculated as days since piercing divided by total expected healing days, capped at 100%. It is an estimate based on standard healing timelines and individual healing rates may vary.

### 9. Can I change my piercing jewellery during Phase 2?
No. The tool advises against changing jewellery during Phase 2 because the fistula looks healed externally but is still forming internally. Jewellery changes should wait until Phase 3 or full healing.

### 10. What does "unusual discharge" mean in the tool's context?
The tool distinguishes between normal lymph fluid (clear or white "crusties") and concerning discharge. Yellow, green, or foul-smelling discharge is flagged as unusual and the tool advises returning to sterile saline only and seeking medical assessment.
